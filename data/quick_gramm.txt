QUICK SPANISH MORPHOLOGY GRAMMAR GUIDE
=======================================

This is a simplified guide for Spanish morphological analysis.
The rules described here are basic and can be extended in the future.

PART OF SPEECH CATEGORIES
--------------------------

1. NOUNS (Sustantivos)
   - Gender: masculine (-o) or feminine (-a)
   - Number: singular or plural (-s, -es)
   - Examples: gato, gatos, casa, casas

2. VERBS (Verbos)
   - Three conjugations: -ar, -er, -ir
   - Tenses: present, past (pretérito), imperfect, future
   - Person: 1st, 2nd, 3rd (singular and plural)
   
   Regular endings:
   -ar verbs (hablar):
     Present: -o, -as, -a, -amos, -áis, -an
     Preterite: -é, -aste, -ó, -amos, -asteis, -aron
   
   -er verbs (comer):
     Present: -o, -es, -e, -emos, -éis, -en
     Preterite: -í, -iste, -ió, -imos, -isteis, -ieron
   
   -ir verbs (vivir):
     Present: -o, -es, -e, -imos, -ís, -en
     Preterite: -í, -iste, -ió, -imos, -isteis, -ieron

3. ADJECTIVES (Adjetivos)
   - Agree with nouns in gender and number
   - Common endings: -o/-a, -e, consonant
   - Examples: bueno/buena, grande, feliz

4. ARTICLES (Artículos)
   Definite: el, la, los, las
   Indefinite: un, una, unos, unas

5. PRONOUNS (Pronombres)
   Personal: yo, tú, él, ella, nosotros, vosotros, ellos, ellas
   Object: me, te, lo, la, se, nos, os, los, las

6. PREPOSITIONS (Preposiciones)
   a, de, en, con, por, para, sin, sobre, entre, etc.

7. ADVERBS (Adverbios)
   - Manner: -mente suffix (rápidamente, lentamente)
   - Time: ahora, hoy, mañana, siempre, nunca
   - Place: aquí, allí, arriba, abajo

8. CONJUNCTIONS (Conjunciones)
   y, o, pero, porque, aunque, si, cuando, etc.

MORPHEME PATTERNS
-----------------

Common Prefixes:
  - des- (negation): deshacer, desorden
  - re- (repetition): rehacer, revivir
  - pre- (before): predecir, prehistoria
  - anti- (against): antivirus, antibiótico

Common Suffixes:
  - -mente (adverb): rápidamente, claramente
  - -ción/-sión (noun): información, decisión
  - -dad/-tad (noun): libertad, igualdad
  - -oso/-osa (adjective): hermoso, peligroso
  - -ito/-ita (diminutive): gatito, casita
  - -ón/-ona (augmentative): grandón, mujerona

Plural Formation:
  - Add -s if word ends in vowel: casa → casas
  - Add -es if word ends in consonant: árbol → árboles
  - No change for words ending in -s (not stressed): crisis → crisis

BASIC ANALYSIS ROADMAP
----------------------

Step 1: Word Tokenization
  - Split text into individual words
  - Remove punctuation and normalize

Step 2: Morpheme Identification
  - Identify root/stem
  - Identify prefixes
  - Identify suffixes
  - Identify inflectional endings

Step 3: Part of Speech Tagging
  - Determine grammatical category
  - Based on endings, patterns, and context

Step 4: Feature Extraction
  - For nouns: gender, number
  - For verbs: tense, person, number
  - For adjectives: gender, number, degree

IMPLEMENTATION NOTES
--------------------

This is a simplified model. Real Spanish morphology is more complex with:
- Irregular verbs (ser, estar, ir, etc.)
- Stem-changing verbs (e→ie, o→ue, e→i)
- Accent rules and orthographic changes
- Compound words
- Context-dependent disambiguation

Future extensions could include:
- A comprehensive dictionary
- Machine learning for disambiguation
- Support for regional variations
- Compound word analysis
